# Keyword plan, October 2026

## How this was researched, and its limits

- **No search volumes.** No Google Ads account or Keyword Planner export was available, and the free KeywordTool.io guest endpoint (`https://mcp.keywordtool.io/guest`) returns keyword text only (volume needs the paid plan). The Google Ads MCP server reads campaign data only and has no keyword-research tool.
- **What was used instead.** UK Google autocomplete suggestions (what people actually type) from KeywordTool.io's guest endpoint, plus public UK cost guides and vendor releases for context. Autocomplete confirms a phrase is searched; it does not rank phrases by volume.
- **To add volumes.** Export a Keyword Planner CSV (UK, English) for the target phrases below, or upgrade KeywordTool.io, then re-rank. Until then treat priority as intent and fit, not volume.
